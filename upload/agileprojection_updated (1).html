Program Details & Curriculum

Embedded Systems Internship

This four-week live program gives engineering students a structured introduction to embedded firmware development. Starting from bare-metal C programming, you'll progress to peripheral interfacing and conclude with a complete RTOS-based project.

Sessions are held daily (2 hours) by industry mentors with hands-on lab assignments after every session. A doubt-clearing session is available every alternate day, plus recorded playbacks for revision.

Week-by-Week Curriculum

  • Week 1C Programming Fundamentals, Memory Model, Bit Manipulation, GCC Toolchain setup
  • Week 2Microcontroller Architecture (ARM Cortex-M), GPIO, UART, Timers & Interrupts
  • Week 3SPI, I2C, ADC/DAC, Sensor interfacing, Debugging with Logic Analyser
  • Week 4FreeRTOS fundamentals, Task scheduling, Semaphores, Final project integration & demo

✅ What You'll Build

  • Sensor data logger with UART output
  • Real-time clock & display system
  • Multi-sensor IoT node with RTOS
  • Complete project documentation & demo video

🎁 Perks & Deliverables

  • Merit-based digital certificate
  • Project source code on GitHub
  • Performance-based goodie packs & vouchers
  • LinkedIn recommendation from mentors
  • Access to recorded sessions for 3 months

📋 Eligibility

  • BE / B.Tech students (3rd – 8th semester)
  • Branches: ECE, EEE, EI, CSE, IT, Mech
  • Basic C programming knowledge preferred
  • Laptop with internet connection required

Internet of Things (IoT) Internship

Build a complete end-to-end IoT solution from sensor to cloud. This four-week program bridges hardware and software — you'll wire up sensors, write firmware, connect to the cloud, and visualise data on a live dashboard.

Ideal for students who enjoy both electronics and software. No prior IoT experience required — just a willingness to build and experiment.

Week-by-Week Curriculum

  • Week 1IoT ecosystem overview, Arduino / ESP32 basics, Python scripting, Breadboard prototyping
  • Week 2Sensors & actuators, DHT22, LDR, PIR, Serial & Wi-Fi communication, MQTT protocol
  • Week 3Cloud platforms (AWS IoT / Firebase), REST APIs, real-time data streaming, JSON payloads
  • Week 4Dashboard creation, alert systems, final project build, demo & presentation to industry panel

✅ What You'll Build

  • Smart environment monitor (temp / humidity / air)
  • Real-time cloud dashboard with charts
  • SMS / email alert system on threshold breach
  • End-to-end project report & demo

🎁 Perks & Deliverables

  • Merit-based digital certificate
  • Complete project on GitHub
  • Cloud deployment walkthrough guide
  • Performance-based vouchers & goodies
  • 90-day recorded session access

📋 Eligibility

  • BE / B.Tech students (3rd – 8th semester)
  • Branches: ECE, CSE, EEE, IT, Mech
  • Basic programming (any language) helpful
  • Laptop with internet + Arduino kit (optional)

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before applying. If your question isn't here, reach out — we reply within 24 hours.

Is the internship really free? Are there any hidden charges?
+
Yes, completely free. There is no registration fee, no course fee, no certificate fee, and no hidden charges of any kind. AgileProjection's mission is to make quality internship programs accessible to every engineering student in India, regardless of financial background.
Will my college accept this certificate for the internship credit requirement?
+
Our certificates are accepted by 500+ engineering colleges across India and are designed to meet AICTE and most university internship mandate requirements. We recommend checking with your college's academic section first — most affiliated colleges accept them without any issue. We can also provide a letter of undertaking if your college needs one.
What equipment or software do I need?
+
You need a laptop (Windows / Mac / Linux) with a stable internet connection. All software tools used (GCC toolchain, Arduino IDE, VS Code, etc.) are free and open-source. For IoT track, having an Arduino Uno or ESP32 kit is helpful but not mandatory — simulated environments are available for students who don't have hardware.
What are the session timings? Can I attend if I have college classes?
+
Sessions are typically scheduled in the evenings (6 PM – 8 PM IST) on weekdays, and Saturday mornings for lab sessions. This is designed to accommodate students who have daytime college schedules. All sessions are recorded and available within 2 hours for those who can't attend live.
I'm in my 2nd year. Can I still apply for the technical internships?
+
Our technical internships (Embedded and IoT) are recommended for students from 3rd semester onwards, as they assume basic programming knowledge. However, exceptionally motivated 2nd-year students with some C / Python exposure are welcome to apply — mention your programming background in the form and our team will assess your fit.
How is the certificate issued? Is it digital or physical?
+
Certificates are issued digitally (PDF with a unique verification QR code) within 7 days of completing the program. They can be downloaded, printed, and shared on LinkedIn directly. A physical certificate can also be requested for a nominal courier charge. All certificates are verifiable online via our portal.
What if I miss a few sessions? Will I still get the certificate?
+
Certificates are merit-based and require completing at least 80% of sessions (or watching recordings) and submitting the final project. Missing 1–2 sessions is fine as long as you catch up via recordings. If you anticipate a longer absence, reach out to your mentor early — we work with students to find solutions wherever possible.
Can non-engineering students apply?
+
Our technical programs (Embedded, IoT) are designed specifically for engineering students. The Campus Ambassador Program is open to any college student who has strong communication skills and is passionate about outreach — engineering degree not required for CAP.
